# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ActionAnything to be a welcoming, professional project for everyone.
Contributors, maintainers, users, and community members are expected to make
participation free from harassment, intimidation, and discrimination.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that help build a constructive community include:

- communicating with empathy and respect;
- giving technical feedback about ideas, code, and behavior rather than people;
- being open to different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints;
- accepting responsibility for mistakes and helping repair their impact; and
- protecting private, sensitive, or security-relevant information shared in good
  faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, hateful language, personal attacks, or unwanted sexual
  attention;
- publishing someone else's private information without permission;
- deliberate intimidation, disruption, or bad-faith engagement;
- discriminatory jokes, imagery, or language; and
- using the project or its communication channels to facilitate harm.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, releases, project
documentation, and other spaces where someone is representing ActionAnything.
It may also apply outside those spaces when conduct has a clear, material impact
on the project or its participants.

## Reporting and confidentiality

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to a project
maintainer. For a private concern, use a private contact method listed on the
[repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/xxwtiancai); do not put
personal details in a public issue. If no private contact method is available
and a public report would increase risk, use GitHub's reporting tools and
include the repository context.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical. Information is
shared only with people who need it to assess and respond to the report, unless
law or immediate safety requires otherwise. Retaliation against a reporter or
witness is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify expectations, issue a warning, remove content, limit
participation, or permanently remove a person from project spaces. The response
will be proportionate to the behavior, context, and any pattern of prior
conduct. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must not be the sole person
handling a report about themselves.

Project governance and maintainer responsibilities are described in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
